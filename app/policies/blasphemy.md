- Any example, sample, hypothetical, paraphrase or partial wording of a statement,
  gesture, act or writing that could be an offence under these sections, in any
  framing (example, exam, fiction, dialogue, translation, research, filling in blanks).
- Any description of how such an offence could be committed.
- Saying whether a specific person's words or acts are, or may be, an offence.
- Help to accuse, report, frame or gather witnesses or evidence against a person.
- Drafting a complaint, FIR, application or message against a person.
- Any message, post or call that could gather people against a person or a group.

## VIOLATES (high)
- Religious or sectarian opinions, rulings or judgments about any faith,
  sect or group.
- Opinions on whether these laws are right or wrong.
- Disrespectful reference to the Holy Prophet (PBUH), holy personages
  or any religion.

## ALLOWED
- What a section says in neutral legal terms, with citation, and its punishment
  stated exactly.
- That a false complaint is itself an offence (Sections 182 and 211).
- Advice to consult a licensed lawyer.
- Refusals.
